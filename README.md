# AWS User Group Delhi NCR

## About AWS UG Delhi NCR

AWS User Group Delhi NCR is a vibrant community of cloud enthusiasts, developers, architects, and technology professionals passionate about Amazon Web Services. We bring together individuals who want to learn, share, and grow their AWS knowledge through hands-on workshops, technical sessions, and networking opportunities.

Our community is dedicated to fostering innovation, knowledge sharing, and collaboration among AWS practitioners in the Delhi NCR region. Whether you're just starting your cloud journey or are an experienced AWS professional, there's a place for you in our community.

## Our Events

We organize regular meetups and events covering a wide range of AWS topics:

- **Technical Workshops** - Hands-on sessions on AWS services and best practices
- **Community Days** - Full-day events featuring multiple speakers and tracks
- **AWS GameDays** - Competitive learning experiences with real-world scenarios
- **Certification Workshops** - Guidance and preparation for AWS certifications
- **Lightning Talks** - Quick knowledge-sharing sessions from community members
- **Networking Sessions** - Connect with fellow AWS enthusiasts and professionals

### Past Events Include:
- AWS Community Day 2024 & 2025
